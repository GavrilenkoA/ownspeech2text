# Benchmark inputs: sources and licences

Everything under `bench/` is bundled into the APK and read by `BenchInputs.bundled()`. Total size about 1.6 MB.

## ASR clips (`asr/`)

Raw PCM16 little-endian, 16 kHz, mono, no header. References in `asr/clips.json` are the FLEURS `raw transcription`
column, unchanged.

Source: FLEURS (Google; Conneau et al., 2022), dev split, licence **CC BY 4.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Downloaded from
`https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs/resolve/70bb2e84b976b7e960aa89f1c648e09c59f894dd/data/<lang>/audio/dev.tar.gz`
(archive sha256: `ru_ru` `4dc533cd0f312f749bd6d0fe968f3183288d938e07ed97dc141d9f10570457f9`, `en_us`
`2658fda72f199e12676ecac9415094667a4e14e149b146e568ea00b2a2f0954c`). The WAV members are IEEE float32; they were
converted to PCM16 with `round(x * 32767)`, clamped. No gain change, no filtering.

FLEURS has no code-switched utterances, so the mixed clip is a Russian utterance followed by an English one, with
about 0.7 s of the recordings' own silence between them (the trailing silence of part 1 was cut at 5.58 s to keep the
clip near 12 s). Its reference is the two transcriptions joined by a space.

Selection rule: dev split, no digits in the transcription (the WER normaliser keeps numerals as written, so "2024" and
"две тысячи двадцать четыре" would differ; it folds `ё` to `е` like the asr-ocr spike), no Latin letters in the Russian ones, 10-12 s (or about 6 s for the halves of the mixed clip),
peak level above -11 dBFS where the split offers one (many FLEURS recordings are very quiet).

Host check (sherpa-onnx 2.0.1 CPU, int8, Silero VAD v5 segments with a 0.5 s minimum pause, as the app's batch path):
GigaAM v3 RNN-T `ru` 0.0 % WER; Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 `ru` 4.3 %, `en` 10.5 %, `mixed` 3.7 %. Decoding the mixed clip
as one window instead drops its English half (51.9 %), the behaviour ADR-025 describes.

## OCR pages (`ocr/`)

Synthetic renders of the OCR golden suite (`ml/ocr/golden`, generated by `ml/ocr/golden.py`; no third-party content,
licence-free). The PNGs are byte-identical copies; `ocr/pages.json` holds the golden's `exclude_rects` and its final
`ocr` lines (pipeline `ocr.v2`).

## LLM fixed cleanup case (`llm/case.json`)

- `context`: the ctx v2 object of `prompts/examples/ctxv2_chat_ru.input.json` (a synthetic Telegram chat, about 200
  tokens rendered), unchanged.
- `transcript`: a 60-word Russian dictation written for this benchmark (project-owned text, no third-party source) that
  answers that chat, with a filler and spoken numbers, as raw ASR output (lowercase, no punctuation).
- The system prefix is not stored here: the runner renders the model's real profile prefix
  (`LlmTraits.promptProfile` + `template`) with the app's `PromptRenderer`.
